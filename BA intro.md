What is a Business Analyst?

A Business Analyst (BA) is the bridge between a business's problems/goals and the technical or process solutions that solve them. They figure out what an organization actually needs, then translate that into requirements that developers, process owners, or vendors can act on.

What a BA actually does, day to day
Understands the problem — talks to stakeholders to find out what's broken or what opportunity exists (e.g. "invoices keep getting stuck on hold, why?")
Gathers requirements — through interviews, workshops, document review, observation
Analyzes current vs. future state — maps how a process works today ("as-is") and designs how it should work ("to-be")
Documents requirements — writes BRDs (Business Requirement Documents), FRDs (Functional Requirement Documents), user stories, process flows
Bridges business and tech teams — makes sure developers/IT understand what the business needs, and the business understands what's technically feasible
Validates solutions — tests that what was built actually solves the original problem (UAT — user acceptance testing)
Manages stakeholders — keeps departments aligned, manages expectations, resolves conflicting requirements
Supports decision-making — uses data analysis (SQL, Excel, Power BI) to back up recommendations with evidence
